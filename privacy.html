Njal

Njal is personal assistant software that I run for myself. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else. It connects to my own Google accounts through Google's OAuth system to help me manage my own email, for example to find invoices and prepare draft replies.

Data accessed through Google APIs (email content and metadata, drafts, and basic Gmail settings) belongs to me and is used only to perform tasks I ask for. It is processed on my own computer, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties, except that the AI model provider I use processes the parts of a task it needs to complete it. I can revoke Njal's access at any time in my Google Account settings.

Njal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
